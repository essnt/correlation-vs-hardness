# AI 使用披露（AI Disclosure）

本文件随公开仓库快照发布，提供中英双版；论文 Reproducibility Statement
所指的完整披露见随快照发布的本文件。

## 英文版（English version）

> **Declaration on the use of AI tools.** The author conceived the research
> question, designed the interventional methodology and every experiment,
> made all strategic decisions (preregistered hypotheses, amendment handling,
> and kill-line decisions), specified the acceptance criteria against which
> every result was judged, and takes full responsibility for the work. AI
> tools (GLM-based coding agent, ZCode environment) were used in an assistive
> capacity, strictly under the author's direction and step-by-step
> verification, for literature search and citation verification, assistance
> with implementing the generators/solvers/statistical pipelines, execution
> of the experiment matrix, and assistance with manuscript drafting and LaTeX
> preparation. No output entered the permanent record on AI authority alone:
> results were accepted only after passing an author-directed verification
> process (machine-checkable gates, deterministic regeneration from raw
> data, and the prespecification-vs-implementation audit), all claims,
> numbers, and citations were verified against primary sources, and the
> audit trail, all raw data, and the full decision history are released in
> the public repository so that every claim can be checked independently.
> No generative AI tool is an author of this work.
>
> A Zenodo record (DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.22777748 — concept DOI, resolving to
> the latest version; the first published version is 10.5281/zenodo.22777749)
> containing the compact version of the paper and the full repository
> snapshot is referenced as a related identifier.
